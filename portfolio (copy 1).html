about me

I’ve spent more than 20 years turning business opportunities into ideas people can understand, feel and remember.

My career has taken me through Leo Burnett, FP7 McCann, Impact BBDO, Havas and Dubai World Trade Centre, working across banking, aviation, retail, FMCG, government, technology and large-scale experiences.

I grew up through design and art direction, which means craft is still very much part of how I think. But over time the role became bigger: finding the idea, shaping the story, building the visual world, guiding teams and production, and making sure the final work still carries the thought that started it. That combination is probably the simplest way to describe me today. I can move upstream into strategy and creative direction, then move back into the detail when typography, a frame, a character, a film treatment or a visual system needs attention.

Born and raised in Dubai, I bring a multicultural point of view shaped by global agency culture and more than two decades of making work across the Middle East and beyond.

The work has picked up recognition along the way, including WARC, Dubai Lynx, Clio, Gerety, Caples, The One Show and MMA SMARTIES. Awards matter. But the part I still enjoy most is that moment when a complicated brief suddenly becomes a simple idea.

Still curious. Still making.
Still looking for the next one.

After years inside the creative industry, I kept seeing the same thing: good thinking getting fragmented between strategy decks, creative development, production pressure and tight timelines. That led me to build VEXA.

VEXA is an AI-powered Brand Operating System designed to connect strategy, human insight, creative thinking and execution in one structured process. For me, it is less about replacing creativity and more about protecting the conditions that help better creativity happen.

It is the natural next chapter of how I work:
human judgment first, technology helping us get faster.
